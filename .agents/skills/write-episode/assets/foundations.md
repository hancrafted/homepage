# Foundations

One durable principle, shown working, ending in a Takeaway. 10–20 minutes. A takeaway is required, and the Publish bar applies in full.

Durable material is pre-hype canon — separation of concerns, design by contract, decomposition — translated for an audience that is not engineering. Showing today's agentic practice as an old idea wearing new clothes is what a sceptical audience needs to hear, and it is what gives the Episode shelf life.

## Beats

```markdown
## Definitions

## The practice they already run

## The model they are running

## The question that breaks it

## The explanation

## Shown working

## Where the method stops applying

## Where the analogy breaks down

## What to do
```

**Definitions.** Every term the Episode needs in order to be understood, defined once. It opens with the fixed lede sentence and carries no prose of its own beyond it. `episode-format.md`'s `## Definitions` holds the form, the fixed sentence, and where a Subject-domain-specific definition goes. Cite it: what a term officially means is a claim about the world, not the author's own framing.

**The practice they already run.** Two things in sequence. **The hook** — one to three lines of something a fifteen-year-old inhabits: everyday life, pop culture, dating, the water cooler, with no professional experience assumed. Then **the analogy**, the line returned to at every major turn. It may come from anywhere the audience can picture, including a domain nobody in it has worked in — what it needs is that the audience can stand inside it, not that anyone has been paid to. The hook may pivot into the analogy or be the analogy's first line; neither takes a Beat of its own, because a Beat for three lines is structure charging rent. The analogy is the entry point, not a decoration laid over a simplified version of the technical thing. Exempt from citation.

**The model they are running.** Name the outdated mental model out loud. The audience uses agentic tools daily; the gap is a stale model, not missing knowledge — so this beat surfaces the wrong model rather than explaining around it. Cite it: this is a claim about what people believe.

**The question that breaks it.** One question the stale model answers wrongly. This is the beat that makes the explanation land, and skipping it turns the Episode into a clear lecture that leaves the misconception intact while raising confidence.

**The explanation.** The durable principle, named the way the audience will recognise, then connected to what they are already doing with agentic tools. Cite it, and resolve the misconception explicitly — raising a wrong model without closing it leaves an audience with more misconceptions than saying nothing would have.

**Shown working.** A worked example, simple before complex. Cite it.

**Where the method stops applying.** The conditions the principle needs to hold, and where it stops being the right method at all — a boundary, not a caveat. This is a claim about the world, so cite it. A rule stated without its limit becomes a slogan and then gets ignored.

**Where the analogy breaks down.** Where the analogy stops mapping, and why that exception is deliberate. The analogy and the method fail for different reasons — the analogy stops mapping, the method stops applying — and one heading covering both hides whichever the author found harder to write. This is the most-skipped step in analogy-led teaching, and skipping it produces analogy-induced misconceptions. The analogy is the author's own, so this beat asserts nothing about the world and carries no citation.

**What to do.** Reach back to the analogy to motivate it — _you wouldn't do this in the analogy, so what would you do here?_ Then name at least one action with its object already supplied, not a procedure for deriving one: _open the deal you are least sure of and write down the one date you do not control_, not _find the first thing outside your control_. Attemptable this week, needing no permission and no new tool. A short checklist is permitted where the material suits one, and required nowhere. Each step meets the same test a single action does — its object supplied, not a step for deriving one. Where the action differs by Subject domain it goes under that Subject domain's `###` heading; where one action serves all of them it belongs in the lede. Exempt from citation.

One analogy carried the length of an Episode is worth more than three good ones used once each.
