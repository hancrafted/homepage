# Short

One to two minutes of opinion, or a teaser for an Episode in a longer Format. A takeaway is optional. Exempt from both Publish bar tests — the only exemption there is — and exempt from the theory/hands-on boundary.

A Short accepts a shorter shelf life. That is the trade it exists to make: it is the cheapest Format to produce and it feeds traffic into the durable Episodes.

## Beats

```markdown
## The claim

## Why it is not obvious

## The turn
```

**The claim.** The opinion, stated flat. Cite it if it rests on a fact; a claim about how something feels to work with needs no source.

**Why it is not obvious.** What makes this worth ninety seconds rather than a sentence.

**The turn.** Where it leaves the listener. A takeaway is welcome but not required — in an opinion Short the absence of one is the point, and that is the named exception to the rule that every piece ends in something actionable.

## No misconception beat

A Short does not open by surfacing a wrong mental model, even though Foundations and Teardown both do.

The gains from misconception-led teaching come from the **refutation**, not from raising the wrong model. A video that introduces a concept via misconceptions and does not resolve them properly leaves viewers holding more misconceptions and fewer correct conceptions than a plain correct explanation, while feeling equally clear. Ninety seconds rarely has room to close one, so a misconception-led Short reliably produces that harmful case.

If a wrong model is genuinely the subject, the material wants a Foundations Episode instead.
